# RecoKit

## AI product recommendations for e-commerce

RecoKit helps online stores recommend relevant products from the first day of a catalog — including new products with little or no sales history.

### For PrestaShop

Install the free [RecoKit PrestaShop module](https://github.com/recokit/prestashop-module/releases/latest) to add:

- similar and complementary products;
- bundles and product comparison;
- recommendations in the cart and add-to-cart modal;
- direct add-to-cart actions from recommendation cards.

The module connects your store to the RecoKit cloud service. Installation and account association are automatic.

### Privacy by design

Store data is isolated per shop. Sales lines are used for that shop's product associations and are not used to train a shared global model. Personal customer contact and payment details are not required for recommendation processing.
